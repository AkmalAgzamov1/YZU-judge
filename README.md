# YZU-Judge

YZU-Judge is an online judge and CPE preparation platform designed to help students in Taiwan, especially YZU students, understand and prepare for the **Collegiate Programming Examination (CPE)**.

## Why I Started This Project

I noticed that many students struggle with the CPE exam, and some may even need to stay at university for a fifth or sixth year.

Depending on the department and graduation requirements, students may need to solve at least two problems in a single CPE exam or three problems across separate exams.

However, many freshmen and even sophomores have little or no idea what CPE is, how it works, or how to prepare for it.

I started YZU-Judge to help students get familiar with CPE, practice programming problems, and prepare for the exam with more confidence.
